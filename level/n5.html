    Common questions about N5

    How long is the JLPT N5 exam?

    90 minutes in all. The papers here run to the same clock, section by section, so a practice sitting costs what the real one does.

    What score do you need to pass N5?

    80 out of 180 — and every scored section has its own minimum as well. One weak section fails the paper however high the total, which is why the papers here are marked section by section.

    How many kanji and words does N5 need?

    Around ~100 kanji and ~800 words. The JLPT stopped publishing its lists in 2010, so every figure you will see quoted is an estimate, this one included.

    Are the N5 papers here free?

    Yes, all 18 of them, with no account and nothing to pay. 18 are written for this site; the rest are archived sittings, kept so you can sit a real one.

    Can I practise N5 listening?

    Yes. All 432 listening questions on this level carry their script, and where the original recording is lost your own device reads that script aloud — so a listening section is never a dead page.
