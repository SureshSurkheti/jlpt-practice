How this book was made

The listening transcripts of 29 real N2 papers (2010-07 to 2024-12) were read end to end: 877 questions, 262,811 characters. The narrator's set-up line — 男おとこの人ひとと女おんなの人ひとが話はなしています — was then thrown away, because it repeats a fixed formula 877 times and would drown everything else. What was left is 6,875 lines of actual dialogue, 202,259 characters. Every candidate phrase was matched against that. A phrase is in this book only if the papers use it; the figure beside each entry is how many lines it appears in, across how many separate sittings. The example under it is a real line from a named paper.

The paper

What the five sections ask of you

श्रवण परीक्षाका पाँच भाग — कुन भागले के माग्छ

問題1 · 課題かだい理解りかい

Task understanding — 145 transcripts held

You hear the question first. Someone will be told to do something — the mark is for what they do first.

問題2 · ポイント理解りかい

Point understanding — 169 transcripts held

You hear the question first, then a pause, then the talk. Usually why. Several reasons are named; one is the speaker's own.

問題3 · 概要がいよう理解りかい

Gist understanding — 144 transcripts held

No question in advance. You must hold the speaker's attitude in your head until the question arrives.

問題4 · 即時そくじ応答おうとう

Quick response — 321 transcripts held

One short line, three short replies, no time to think. The reply answers the move, not the vocabulary.

問題5 · 統合とうごう理解りかい

Integrated understanding — 95 transcripts held

A long talk, then two or three people compare it. You are tracking who wants what.

The one habit worth building

In 問題1 and 問題2 the question is read before the talk. Use that. Decide what you are listening for, then listen only for it. Most lost marks at N2 are not vocabulary — they are a candidate who understood every word and answered a different question.

問題1 र 問題2 मा प्रश्न पहिले नै सुनाइन्छ। त्यसैले पहिले के खोज्ने हो निर्णय गर्नुहोस्, अनि त्यही मात्र सुन्नुहोस्।
